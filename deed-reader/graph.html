      The Hopper: everything enters here first

      Every candidate (a link, a source lead, a correction, an identity) enters the Hopper in its proposer's words, signed and dated. It reaches the graph only through two gates. Nothing is changed or deleted: a revision, a comment, a score or a decision is added beside the item, and a set-aside item stays, with its reason.
