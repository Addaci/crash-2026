How this form works (2.1)

  1. Pick a deed on the left. Its scans are the tabs above the image. Use left half / right half for two-page openings.
  2. Transcribe on the right: one manuscript line per text line, spelling as written. The toolbar inserts the marks and Dutch letters.
  3. Help is welcome and recorded. For the line the cursor is on, tick M (machine text used), C (model consulted), G (glossary or dictionary), or X (not read in the ink). Showing the machine text is recorded with its time. A line you read in the ink is P-H, with the help recorded beside it; a line marked X is not.
  4. HTR (the tab beside Transcribe): the machine text of the scan on the left, where it is on disk, with who made it, by what model, when and how it was fetched. It is better than nothing for Dutch, French and other hands, and a check on your own reading; it is not a transcription (basis P-MUC). Show HTR below my text puts it under your text, numbered. It is read-only: fix (on a line, on hover) keeps a correction of a small glitch beside the original, with your name and the time; the file itself is never changed. Deeds with HTR are marked “HTR” in the list. Translation: in the HTR tab, choose HTR + translation (each line under its Dutch line) or Synthesis (deed), and your language. Translations are a model’s (basis I), made as files on request in a model’s chat, and say whether they are still current. The grey bar between the image and the right-hand side can be dragged; double-click it to reset. A− / A+ in the HTR tab change the size of the Dutch and English there only.
  5. Search (tab): this deed or all deeds; choose the layers (your transcriptions, HTR, translation, synthesis, notes and entities, the index); spellings also finds near spellings and the known variants listed in the data. Exact hits come first, then ink-based layers first. Click a hit to open the deed, scan and line.
  6. Ask a model: draw a box on the image round the words in doubt (Draw box), then Ask a model about this line and choose the model from the list. The question goes to the clipboard (paste it into that model’s chat) and to the log in the Ask a model tab, with the model’s name. Paste the answer back there and type your ruling. For many questions: Save for a batch of questions, then Write query file for one model; that model writes an answer file, which you import. On the team server, the cluster’s own models (marked cluster in the list) are asked by the server itself: choose one and click Ask … now; the box is cut out of the scan and sent with the question, and the answer arrives in the Ask a model tab, credited to that model.
  7. Deed: index & notes holds the archive's index record (basis A, never overwritten), your own readings of language, type and date, English notes on the whole deed, page notes (one box per scan) and the entity check table. To add an entity, select its words in the text and click Selection → entity in the line bar; then choose its kind.
  8. Saving. Everything is kept in this browser as you type. Connect working folder (pick the deed-reader folder) saves each deed as a file in readings/<transcriber>/. Without it, Export all and Save scan .txt go to Downloads: move them into deed-reader/exports/. These folders stay on your computer or the cluster and are never committed.
  9. Team server (when the form is opened from your personal link, not from a file): your name comes from the link and cannot be changed; your work is kept in this browser and also on the server, where every save is added and nothing is overwritten (the status line says when). Under your transcription, Also read by lists the other readers with text on the open scan; Show opens their text, read-only. You never change their work, nor they yours.
